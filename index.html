The club

Excellent courts.
Friendlier than you'd expect.

Cheam Squash Club shares a warm, unpretentious clubhouse feel with a genuinely competitive edge — three well-maintained heated courts host everything from a first-ever lesson to Surrey Cup league nights.

Courts

Well-maintained, heated year-round, and busy without ever feeling impossible to book — used for squash, racketball and casual knock-abouts alike.

Club

Squash and racketball share the courts; an internal table tennis league runs alongside with its own tournaments through the year.

Open to all

From complete beginners to Surrey County players — the club is built around all levels of ability, not just the sharp end of the ladder.

Find your level

Squash is graded
by the ball you play with.

Every squash ball carries a coloured dot that tells you how it plays — livelier and more forgiving through to tournament-slow. It's a genuinely useful map for where to start.

Blue dot

Just starting out

A livelier, higher-bouncing ball built for first swings and rallies that actually go on. Junior and adult beginner sessions run through the club and with our coach.

Red dot

Improvers

Once rallies are consistent, a slightly slower ball rewards accuracy over power. This is where most of our Saturday session regulars sit.

Single yellow

Club standard

The standard ball for most club and league players — slower off the wall, unforgiving of loose length, and exactly what our internal ladder is played on.

Double yellow

Advanced & squad

The competition-standard ball — barely bounces until it's warm. Played by our Surrey Cup squads and anyone chasing a county ranking.
